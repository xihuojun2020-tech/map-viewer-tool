Han orientaux 140 (2017-01-24), by Zunkir.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Han_orientaux_140.svg
CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Base by Kanguole: Eastern China blank relief map.svg, CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eastern_China_blank_relief_map.svg
The base declares equirectangular projection, longitude 99.5 to 125.7 degrees,
latitude 18 to 42.5 degrees. We extracted vector boundary lines and transcribed
thirteen province labels into Chinese; foreign source text and artwork are not
embedded in the application. Source lines are clipped only at the original
image extent, never closed into complete provinces using the image rectangle.
Derivative boundary data are CC BY-SA 4.0. The original SVG and metadata are
preserved in data/references and research. This is an approximate circa-140 CE
reconstruction with cropped western/northeastern coverage; no cadastral or
county-level accuracy is asserted. It is separate from the 219 CE source below.

The source author's stated references are Rafe de Crespigny, A Biographical
Dictionary of Later Han to the Three Kingdoms (23-220 AD), 2007, p.1310, and
The Cambridge History of China, vol.1, 1986, pp.252-253. These references do not
mean this project has obtained and reviewed the full books.

secondary-source checks, not claims of independently surveyed ancient city sites.
